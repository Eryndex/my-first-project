## How we work

1. **Understand** — clarify the business need, users, constraints, and desired outcome.
2. **Scope** — agree deliverables, assumptions, milestones, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria.
3. **Build** — work in reviewable increments with practical, transparent communication.
4. **Handover** — provide the agreed code, documentation, and knowledge transfer; follow-on support is scoped separately.

Every engagement begins with a technical review and written scope. Final deliverables, timelines, and service levels are agreed per project.
